2. Summary

  • Reedborn plays fully offline and never asks you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, location (GPS), microphone or camera data.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports are switched on only if you choose "Allow analytics". You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy.
  • Ads are optional rewarded videos. Google's consent form asks for your choice where the law requires it.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your data and do not build profiles of you.

3. Data stored only on your device

Your save game, settings, unlocked items and your privacy choices are stored locally in the app's private storage (Capacitor Preferences). They do not leave your device through us. Android's automatic backup may copy app data to your Google account if you have backup turned on. This is controlled by Google and your device settings. Uninstalling the app deletes the local data.

Photo mode creates an image only when you press the share or save button. The image goes to the app you choose. We do not receive it.

4. Data processed when you use online features

4.1 Gameplay analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), only with your consent

  • What: game events such as life started or ended, pond, score, days survived, chicks raised, tutorial progress, pond upgrades, whether a rewarded ad was started or completed, and which in-app product was bought. We also process the app version, platform, device model, operating system version, language, a random app-instance ID, and an approximate region that Google derives from the IP address. We do not send names, free text or precise location. Analytics is configured not to collect the advertising ID.
  • Purpose: to understand which parts of the game work, to balance difficulty and to fix problems.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 25(1) TDDDG for storing and reading identifiers on your device). You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings > Privacy > Anonymous analytics. Withdrawal does not affect processing that happened before it.
  • Retention: event-level data is deleted after 2 months (Google Analytics retention setting). Reports that are aggregated and anonymous may be kept longer.

4.2 Crash reports and performance data (Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Performance Monitoring)

  • What: crash and error traces, the app state at the time (app version, device model, OS version, memory and disk status, orientation), a random Crashlytics installation ID, and performance timings such as app start time and loading time.
  • Purpose: to find and fix crashes and slowdowns, and to keep the app stable and secure.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a stable and secure app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Crash reporting needs a random identifier stored on your device, so we only switch it on when you have allowed analytics (consent under § 25(1) TDDDG). If you choose "No thanks" or turn analytics off, no crash or performance reports are sent. You may object at any time (Art. 21 GDPR) by turning analytics off.
  • Retention: crash data is deleted by Google after 90 days. Performance data follows the Firebase retention schedule.

4.3 Remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config)

  • What: when the app is online, it downloads a small configuration file (for example the limit on rewarded ads per day). This request sends a random Firebase installation ID, the app ID and version, and basic device and language information.
  • Purpose: to adjust settings such as ad frequency limits without an app update.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in operating the app and keeping ad limits fair for all players.
  • Retention: according to Firebase's retention for installation IDs. Configuration requests are not used for analytics.

4.4 Rewarded ads (Google AdMob, with Google's User Messaging Platform)

  • Ads appear only if you choose to watch a rewarded video, for example for bonus Pond Points. They never interrupt gameplay. If you buy "Ad-free" or the "Supporter Pack", no ads are shown.
  • What Google processes: advertising ID, app set ID, IP address (for approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions (for example whether an ad was shown or completed). It also processes diagnostic data used to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Consent: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (UMP) asks before personalised ads or the use of device identifiers for ads. Without consent, Google shows non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Legal basis: your consent for personalised ads and device access (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 25(1) TDDDG). Our legitimate interest in financing a free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) covers offering voluntary rewarded ads.
  • Google acts as a separate controller for parts of ad serving: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

4.5 In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

  • Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We receive only the product ID, the purchase status and a purchase token, which the app uses on your device to unlock the item and to restore purchases. We never receive your name, email or payment details.
  • Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Retention: the unlock is stored on your device. Google keeps transaction records under its own policy and statutory tax rules.

4.6 Google Play Games (optional sign-in for leaderboards)

  • Only if you sign in. Google then gives the app your Play Games player ID and gamer profile (name and avatar), and stores the scores you submit. Whether others can see your scores depends on your Play Games settings.
  • Legal basis: your request to use leaderboards (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • You can sign out and manage your profile in the Google Play Games app.

4.7 Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete them when the request is resolved, at the latest after 2 years, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). You can also withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

How to exercise them:

  • Stop analytics and crash reporting: Settings > Privacy > Anonymous analytics: Off.
  • Delete local game data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Delete data already collected: email us at the address in section 1. Because we do not know who you are, analytics data cannot be linked to you by name. We will help as far as we can identify the data. Otherwise it is deleted automatically after the retention periods above.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in your country of residence or in Germany (Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz, Hintere Bleiche 34, 55116 Mainz).

8. Children

Reedborn is not directed at children under 13. Its target audience is teens and adults (13+). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. In the EEA, people under 16 (or the age set by their country) may only consent to analytics or personalised ads with a parent's approval. The whole game works with "No thanks", without any analytics or personalised ads. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We will update this policy when the app or the law changes. The current version is always available in the app (Settings > Privacy > Privacy policy) and on the store listing. Material changes that need your consent will be asked for again in the app.
